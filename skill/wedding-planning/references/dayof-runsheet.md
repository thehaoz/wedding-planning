# Day-of Run Sheet Template

Build this 8+ weeks out. Fill every contact by W-4. Keep one master version; mark changes "Was HH:MM" so the cascade is visible.

## Principles

- The couple does no setup. A **Day-of Captain** (organised friend, not in many photos) runs the clock and is the single contact for vendors.
- Every item has a time, an owner and a note. No item without an owner.
- Build backward from the legal/ceremony time, then forward to venue clear-out.
- Add 25-30% buffer to ceremonies with many steps. Run a dry run for tea ceremony, procession and family photos.
- Confirm in writing: venue early access, clear-out time, rain backup, vendor arrival times.

## Key contacts table (fill all rows)

Role | Name | Phone. Include: makeup artist, photographer, videographer, emcee(s), AV person, venue manager, Day-of Captain, officiant, florist, cake vendor, live-station vendors, favour vendor, transport.

## Master run sheet columns

Time | What happens | Who | Notes (include "Was ..." on changes)

## Bridal party roles (6-8 people)

1. Day-of Captain: owns the clock, receives vendors, makes the rain call.
2. Bride's attendant: dress, veil, emergency kit, phone/bag.
3. Groom's attendant / ring keeper: rings, marriage documents, family photo usher.
4. Décor lead: loads car, guest tables, photo table.
5. Tea & ceremony lead: tea, tray, cups, clear-down.
6. Favours / DIY booth lead.
7. Reception host: signage, guest book, welcomes guests with ang bao custodian.
8. AV & runner: laptop, projector, music cues, props handout.

Family helpers: ang bao custodians (one per side, trusted relatives), tea ceremony caller (an auntie who knows forms of address), 2 witnesses (21+ if required, bring ID).

## Contingency kit

Safety pins, plasters, stain pen, painkillers, power bank, fans, umbrellas, water, spare phone charger, scissors, tape, cake knife and server, pen for documents, tissues.

## Items checklist

**Décor:** guest book, ang bao boxes, flowers and vases, signboards, easels, table runners, ornaments, door gifts, name cards, table numbers.
**Ceremony:** wedding bands (in box), ring pillow, marriage documents + pen, tea flasks + backup hot water, cups, tea, bubble guns, petals.
**AV:** laptop, HDMI cable (3m+ and extension), speaker, playlist login, slides, trivia slides if used.
**Added late (always forgotten):** cake knife and server, convex mirror or stand, favour station supplies, rain gear.

## Guest-facing data

Pull counts from the guest database. Never retype. Include: total attending by segment (ceremony vs reception), dietary flags by name for the caterer, high chairs/baby chairs, mobility needs.
