# Backward Timeline

Count back from the wedding date (W). Adjust to the couple's culture and format. Dates below are lead times, not laws; popular venues and solemnisers/officiants may need more.

## 10-12 months before (W-52 to W-40 weeks)

- Agree total budget ceiling, who pays what, and top 3 priorities.
- Draft guest list (rough count by side of family).
- Shortlist and **book venue and caterer/banquet** (50% deposits are common; log deposit and balance dates).
- Set the date with family elders (auspicious date, travel, school/work calendars).
- Create the planning workspace: Budget, Payments, Vendors, Tasks, Guests.

## 8-9 months before (W-36 to W-32)

- Book photographer/videographer (best ones fill first).
- Book officiant/solemniser; **verify licence validity for your date on the official portal** and get availability in writing.
- Engagement ring, wedding bands, gold/jewellery: start early, buy in stages, log each purchase.
- Research and shortlist attire.
- Register the marriage / book the legal slot if your country requires lead time.

## 6-7 months before (W-28 to W-24)

- Choose attire; pay deposits. Book hair and makeup (trial included).
- Pick florist/stylist: send the same brief and budget to 3 vendors, compare side by side.
- Order favours (long production times).
- Pre-wedding shoot booking; plan outfits and location.
- Food tasting.

## 4-5 months before (W-20 to W-16)

- Lock guest list; send invitations or save-the-dates.
- Draft the day-of timeline. **Time any ceremony with many steps (tea, speeches, family photos) with a real dry run.**
- Book live stations/cake/entertainment.
- Plan transport for couple, elders, setup crew.
- Recruit bridal party / helper crew and assign roles in writing.

## 3 months before (W-12)

- Track RSVPs in the guest database; collect dietary needs and mobility requirements.
- Finalise menu and brief the caterer on dietary flags.
- Confirm venue early-access time and clear-out time in writing.
- Rain/heat plan for outdoor segments, with the indoor backup confirmed in writing.

## 6-8 weeks before (W-8 to W-6)

- Finalise run sheet v1 and share with bridal party.
- Fill all contact numbers in the run sheet (do not leave placeholders).
- Seating plan draft; place cards/menu cards ordered.
- Confirm witnesses (with ID if required) and officiant order of service.

## 4 weeks before (W-4)

- Final guest numbers to caterer/venue.
- Final fitting(s). Collect rings and attire when ready.
- Pay balances due; request final invoices.
- Prepare ang bao / tips / thank-you envelopes and decide who hands them out.

## 1 week before (W-1)

- Weather watch for outdoor plans; make the rain-plan call rule (who decides, by what time).
- Pack the load-in checklist; charge AV; test slides and playlist.
- Confirm every vendor's arrival time by message and have them reply.
- Rest. Delegate everything on the day to the day-of captain.

## Day before and day of

See `dayof-runsheet.md`.

## Compressed timeline (under 6 months)

1. Week 1: budget, guest estimate, date, **venue + caterer + officiant** all in one go.
2. Week 2: photographer, attire, makeup.
3. Weeks 3-6: invitations out, florist, favours (choose in-stock items), legal registration.
4. Weeks 7-onward: run sheet, helpers, transport, rain plan.
Say clearly which vendors are likely already booked out on short notice, and offer fallback options.
