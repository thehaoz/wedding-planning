# Optional Culture and Locale Modules

Use only what applies. Ask the couple and their families before assuming customs. Verify legal steps against official sources.

## Example: civil solemnisation (Singapore)

- Registration with the Registry of Marriages (ROM) must be done in advance. Confirm current fees and notice periods on the official ROM / Marriage website.
- Solemnisers are licensed. Shortlist against a brief (location, language mix, religion, style, ang bao). **Verify the licence validity covers your date on the official portal** and get date/time confirmation in writing. Book 3-6 months ahead for popular solemnisers.
- Typical items to arrange: two witnesses (with ID), original certificate and marriage registration form kept safe on the day, order of service shared with emcee and photographer, ang bao agreed in advance.
- Outdoor ceremonies: weather backup space and a decision time.

## Chinese tea ceremony

- Build a **serving order** by generation, then paternal side before maternal where families follow that custom (confirm with elders). Capture the correct address terms for each relative.
- Count groups and persons; estimate 2-5 minutes per group. 14 groups took about 40 minutes in the source plan, not 30.
- Roles: tea caller (an auntie who knows forms of address), tea server, tea maker, photographer positioning.
- Items: flasks and backup hot water, tray, cups, tea, list of elders in order.
- Someone who arrives late or is served separately (for example after lunch) needs a standalone slot in the run sheet.

## Betrothal gifts / guo da li

- Typically a set of items agreed between families; track as its own budget category. The source plan spent about 4% of total spend across about 15 line items (dried goods, bird's nest, abalone, cakes, a wallet, catering and decor).
- Ask elders what is expected, then keep the list and receipts together.

## Gold / jewellery (si dian jin and bands)

- Log each purchase separately with weight, price and date; these are large and often spread across months.
- Record where each item is stored after the wedding.

## Ang bao

- Decide amounts for: officiant, helpers, family elders, vendors if customary. Name a trusted custodian per side for received ang bao and move boxes to a family car in stages.
- Budget helper ang bao in advance (the source plan logged about 2% of total spend after the wedding).

## Other cultures

For other traditions, ask: which ceremonies are required, who hosts/pays, who needs to be served or seated first, and what must happen at a fixed time. Build those into the run sheet as timed blocks with owners.
