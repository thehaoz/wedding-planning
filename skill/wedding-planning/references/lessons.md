# Lessons, Audit Checklist, Wrap-up

Derived from reviewing a real planning workspace. Use these to audit a couple's current plan.

## Audit checklist (run monthly, and at W-12, W-6, W-2)

Budget
- [ ] Is there a written total ceiling and per-category planned amount?
- [ ] Does every payment have a status and a due date?
- [ ] Do any two places show different totals for the same vendor? (flag, don't guess)
- [ ] Is there a contingency line and has it been used?
- [ ] Who paid each item? Who is owed?
- [ ] Are tips, helper ang bao, officiant fee and transport budgeted?

Tasks
- [ ] Do 100% of tasks have an owner and due date?
- [ ] Are finished tasks ticked? (Stale task lists stop being trusted)
- [ ] Are "still to confirm" notes in other pages converted into tasks?

Vendors
- [ ] Every vendor has contact, contract/quote link, deposit/balance dates and arrival time
- [ ] Rain and clear-out times confirmed in writing

Guests
- [ ] One guest database; counts on other pages link to it
- [ ] Duplicates and placeholder rows cleaned
- [ ] Each allergy and diet matched to a meal choice and confirmed with the caterer

Run sheet
- [ ] Contacts filled, not placeholders
- [ ] Roles assigned to named people
- [ ] Dry run done for multi-step ceremonies
- [ ] Contingency kit and checklist packed

## Mistakes seen and how to avoid them

1. **No total budget.** Fix: ceiling first.
2. **Ledger and vendor page disagreed by about 12% of total spend.** Fix: one row per payment; reconcile monthly.
3. **Blank payment statuses (about 6% of spend).** Fix: status is required.
4. **Tea ceremony timed at 30 min, was 40 with 14 groups.** Fix: count groups, dry run at W-8, add buffer.
5. **Late discovery of schedule cascade** (+15 min through the afternoon, clear-out moved later and unconfirmed). Fix: confirm clear-out time after any schedule change.
6. **Placeholders in the contact table five weeks out.** Fix: complete by W-4.
7. **Vendor database held 4 of 10+ vendors.** Fix: add every vendor on day of booking.
8. **Task list stopped matching reality.** Fix: one task system; link notes to tasks.
9. **Stale guest counts in the run sheet.** Fix: link to the guest database.
10. **Date typo** (weekday did not match date). Fix: double-check day-of-week on every vendor-facing document.
11. **Dietary conflict left as a note.** Fix: email the caterer and get a reply.
12. **Costs unplanned: helper ang bao, small DIY purchases.** Fix: contingency and a "small purchases" envelope.

## Good habits worth copying

- Research 10+ options against a written brief before choosing (solemniser, stylist).
- Give the couple zero setup duties on the day.
- Show every schedule change as "Was ..." so cascades stay visible.
- Keep dietary and seating detail with the guest record.
- Note budget bands per category on research pages (for example a decor range) so quotes can be compared.

## Post-wedding 30-day wrap-up

- [ ] Confirm all balances paid; collect final invoices and receipts into one folder
- [ ] Return rentals and keys; collect leftovers and the certificate
- [ ] Safe-store rings and marriage documents
- [ ] Thank helpers (gift or note) and vendors (reviews, photo credits)
- [ ] Photographer delivery timeline agreed; back up files in two places
- [ ] Final budget summary: planned vs committed vs actual per category
- [ ] Write a one-page "what I'd do differently" while it's fresh
- [ ] Update any name/status paperwork (ID, bank, insurance) if relevant
